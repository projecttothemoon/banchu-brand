BANCHU BUNNY LOGO KIT

45 artwork variants, each as genuine path-based SVG and PNG, plus web icons.
Open index.html to preview and download each file.

FOLDERS
01-marks: bordered, borderless, outline, one-color and sticker versions.
02-lockups: horizontal and stacked logos with dark or light lettering.
03-wordmarks: text only in navy, cream, black, white and blue.
04-backgrounds: mark and lockups on cream, navy and blue.
05-app-icons: full square 1024px tiles in three colors, with or without bunny border.
06-social: circular avatars and 2400x1260 social banners.
07-web-icons: SVG/ICO favicons, 16–1024px PNG icons, Apple touch icon, maskable icon and web manifest.

USAGE
Use SVG for websites, large displays and print; lettering is converted to paths and needs no installed font.
Use PNG for slides, social platforms and tools that do not accept SVG.
Cream and white assets have actual transparent backgrounds and may be invisible on a white canvas.
The app tiles have square corners so platforms can apply their own corner masks.
The circular avatar has transparency outside its circle.
The favicon and the icon-*.png sizes are the transparent bunny (sky with a navy outline), which reads on light and dark backgrounds. The navy tile versions are in 07-web-icons/tile/. The Apple touch icon and maskable icon stay navy tiles: phones need a solid background.
Keep clear space around the bunny and do not stretch it.

WEB SETUP
Copy the files from 07-web-icons to your public website folder, then add:
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
<link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">
Set the manifest start_url to your app's preferred landing page if needed.

ALIGNMENT
All bunny variants are centered horizontally on the midpoint of the two eye centers, rather than the unequal ears. The eyes retain their original shapes and positions relative to each other. Vertical positioning is unchanged. Transparent favicons use slightly more breathing room so the longer right ear remains fully visible. Wordmark-only artwork is unchanged.

PRODUCTION NOTES
The selected concept was isolated using the built-in image_gen tool, then traced into reusable vector geometry. Flat palette colors replace the generated image texture. All layouts and color variants share the same canonical bunny and wordmark paths. The borderless artwork removes the navy perimeter while retaining the face geometry.
